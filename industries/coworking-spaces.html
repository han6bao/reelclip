Questions from coworking spaces

Do you make video for coworking spaces?

Coworking video that sells the space, the community and the membership. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do coworking spaces need?

Most start with space tours, member stories and event and community content, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Do you shoot drone for properties?

Yes. Aerials can be planned into any property or development shoot where the location allows it.

How much does video for coworking spaces cost?

Projects typically start at $2,000, with custom quotes based on the creative direction, production needs, and final deliverables. Tell us what you're hoping to create and the budget you have in mind, and we'll explore what's possible together.
