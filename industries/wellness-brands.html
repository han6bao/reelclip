Questions from wellness brands

Do you make video for wellness brands?

Wellness brand video that feels calm, credible and real. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do wellness brands need?

Most start with product launches, founder stories and lifestyle content, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

How do you handle patient privacy?

We only film people who have given consent, and we plan shots so no protected information appears on screen.
