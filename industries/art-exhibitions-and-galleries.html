Questions from art exhibitions & galleries

Do you make video for art exhibitions & galleries?

Gallery and exhibition video that brings artwork and openings to a wider audience. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do art exhibitions & galleries need?

Most start with exhibition films, artist interviews and opening night recaps, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Do you work with independent artists?

Yes. Most of our music videos are with independent Washington artists.

How much does video for art exhibitions & galleries cost?

It depends on scope: shoot days, crew, locations and how many deliverables you need. Share your budget range on the project form and we'll design the strongest version that fits it.
