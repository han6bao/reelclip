Questions from community organizations

Do you make video for community organizations?

Video for community organizations that shows the work and the people behind it. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do community organizations need?

Most start with program films, event coverage and volunteer and member stories, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Do you offer captioned and accessible versions?

Yes. Captions and accessible versions are part of our post-production.

How much does video for community organizations cost?

It depends on scope: shoot days, crew, locations and how many deliverables you need. Share your budget range on the project form and we'll design the strongest version that fits it.
