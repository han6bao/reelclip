Questions from concerts & music festivals

Do you make video for concerts & music festivals?

Concert and festival coverage, like Mariah The Scientist at Sarajevo. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do concerts & music festivals need?

Most start with performance recaps, festival highlight films and backstage and artist content, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Do you work with independent artists?

Yes. Most of our music videos are with independent Washington artists.

How much does video for concerts & music festivals cost?

It depends on scope: shoot days, crew, locations and how many deliverables you need. Share your budget range on the project form and we'll design the strongest version that fits it.
