Questions from conventions & trade shows

Do you make video for conventions & trade shows?

Convention and trade show video, from booth content to full show recaps. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do conventions & trade shows need?

Most start with booth and product demos, show floor recaps and interviews and testimonials, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Can you cover several activations at once?

Yes. We scale the crew and direct multiple videographers across venues.

How much does video for conventions & trade shows cost?

It depends on scope: shoot days, crew, locations and how many deliverables you need. Share your budget range on the project form and we'll design the strongest version that fits it.
