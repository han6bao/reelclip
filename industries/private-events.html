Questions from private events

Do you make video for private events?

Private events, parties and celebrations, filmed discreetly and edited beautifully. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do private events need?

Most start with event highlight films, same-week recaps and guest interviews, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Can you film in dark venues?

Yes. Low-light nightlife is a big part of our portfolio, and our grade keeps footage consistent across changing lights.

How much does video for private events cost?

It depends on scope: shoot days, crew, locations and how many deliverables you need. Share your budget range on the project form and we'll design the strongest version that fits it.
