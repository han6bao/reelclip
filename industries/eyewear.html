Questions from eyewear

Do you make video for eyewear?

Eyewear video that shows the frames, the fit and the style. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do eyewear need?

Most start with collection launches, try-on content and optical boutique films, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Can you shoot small products in macro?

Yes. Jewelry, watches, fragrance and accessories get dedicated macro and lighting setups.

How much does video for eyewear cost?

Projects typically start at $2,000, with custom quotes based on the creative direction, production needs, and final deliverables. Tell us what you're hoping to create and the budget you have in mind, and we'll explore what's possible together.
