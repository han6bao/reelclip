Video made for restaurants

Food and drink live or die on texture, light and appetite appeal. We plan for the pour, the sizzle and the first sip, then build the people and the place around them.

We've filmed Jack's BBQ's pitmaster at the smoker, Lula Coffee Co.'s pink cafe and signature drinks, and Chilean Salmon's chef demonstrations. For spirits and tequila brands we bring the same approach to product launches, bar programs, brand activations and distributor content, following your responsible-marketing guidelines and casting talent of legal drinking age.

Questions from restaurants

Do you make video for restaurants?

Restaurant video that fills tables: the food, the room and the people who make it. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do restaurants need?

Most start with signature dish films, opening and new-menu promos and chef and owner stories, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Do you work with tequila and spirits brands?

Yes. We produce launch films, cocktail and bar content, activations and distributor videos for spirits brands, and follow your brand's responsible-marketing and talent-age requirements.

How much does video for restaurants cost?

Projects typically start at $2,000, with custom quotes based on the creative direction, production needs, and final deliverables. Tell us what you're hoping to create and the budget you have in mind, and we'll explore what's possible together.
