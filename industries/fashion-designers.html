Questions from fashion designers

Do you make video for fashion designers?

Films for independent designers that show the vision and the craft behind the collection. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do fashion designers need?

Most start with designer profiles, collection launch films and studio and process stories, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Can you shoot a full collection in one day?

Usually, yes. We schedule by look and location to get hero, detail and social coverage efficiently.

How much does video for fashion designers cost?

Projects typically start at $2,000, with custom quotes based on the creative direction, production needs, and final deliverables. Tell us what you're hoping to create and the budget you have in mind, and we'll explore what's possible together.
