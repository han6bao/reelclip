Questions from luxury retail

Do you make video for luxury retail?

Luxury retail video that brings the in-store experience to every screen. Reelclip is based in Seattle and works across Washington.

What kind of videos do luxury retail need?

Most start with boutique brand films, product showcases and vip event coverage, then add vertical cutdowns for social. We'll recommend the right mix for your goals and budget.

Can you film in our store during open hours?

Yes, with a small crew. For hero shots we usually schedule before opening.

How much does video for luxury retail cost?

Projects typically start at $2,000, with custom quotes based on the creative direction, production needs, and final deliverables. Tell us what you're hoping to create and the budget you have in mind, and we'll explore what's possible together.
