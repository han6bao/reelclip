We drive to Mill Creek regularly. Distance isn't a problem: we're happy to make the trip for the right project. We bring the full brand films process to Mill Creek: planning, crew, filming, editing and delivery.

What's the difference between a brand film and a commercial?

A commercial sells a specific product or offer. A brand film builds trust in who you are, so it keeps working for years on your website, in pitches and in hiring.

Do we need a script?

Not necessarily. Many brand films are built from interviews and observed moments. We'll recommend scripted, documentary or a hybrid based on your story.
