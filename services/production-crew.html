Crew + support

Video production crew for hire in Seattle

For agencies and marketing teams that already have the creative direction and need a reliable team to shoot, direct crews and deliver on deadline.

START A PROJECT →SEE EXAMPLES

Sometimes the idea is done and what you need is execution. Reelclip can produce and direct the shoot, hire and brief additional videographers and editors, and manage the workflow from footage to final delivery.

For TurboTax, Reelclip worked as Production Director across three activations: hiring the crew, assigning each event, filming two of them, and coordinating post so all three videos landed within a week.

What's included

  • Production direction + coordination
  • Videographers and camera operators
  • Audio capture and mic setup
  • Editors and post-production management
  • Crew hiring and briefing
  • Client communication and delivery management

How it works

  1. Brief

    Your creative, shot list, specs and deadlines.

  2. Crew

    We assemble and brief the right people.

  3. Execute

    On-site direction and real-time problem solving.

  4. Hand off

    Organized footage or finished edits, on time.

Typical deliverables

  • Footage delivered to your specs
  • Edits to your agency's brief
  • Organized media handoff
  • On-site production management
