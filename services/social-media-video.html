Social media video production

Cinematic vertical and horizontal content for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and paid social, made to hold attention in the first second.

START A PROJECT →SEE EXAMPLES

Social content has to earn attention fast, and it has to keep coming. We shoot for vertical from the start, write hooks into the edit and plan batches so you leave one shoot day with weeks of posts.

For Peristera we delivered a launch film plus five stylized reels, one for each look. For Laid Back Allure we cut vertical stories from the weekend's events, including the all-white party and the patio.

What's included

  • Vertical-first shooting
  • Hook-driven editing
  • Batch content days
  • Captions + on-screen text
  • Trend-aware formats
  • Paid social ad variations

How it works

  1. Content plan

    Pillars, formats and a posting calendar.

  2. Batch shoot

    Many posts captured in one efficient day.

  3. Edit

    Hooks, pacing, captions and sound for each platform.

  4. Repeat

    Monthly batches that keep your feed consistent.

Typical deliverables

  • Reels / TikToks / Shorts
  • Story sequences
