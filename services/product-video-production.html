Product launches

Product video production + launch campaigns

Product films and short-form assets that make people want the thing, designed for launch day, your product pages and paid social.

LET'S CONNECT →SEE EXAMPLES

Launching something new? We plan the hero film, the teasers and the always-on assets together, so your launch has a consistent look from the first teaser to the last retargeting ad.

We've filmed an apparel brand's first collection, a streetwear jacket launch, a bike brand's hero film and an exotic car rental campaign. Each one got a different approach, because a jacket and a Lamborghini shouldn't be filmed the same way.

What's included

  • Launch campaign planning
  • Styled product + macro cinematography
  • Lifestyle scenes with real people
  • Studio or on-location setups
  • E-commerce and product-page clips
  • Motion graphics + text animation

How it works

  1. Launch plan

    Map every asset to a launch moment: teaser, drop day and after.

  2. Styling

    Props, sets, wardrobe and talent that fit your audience.

  3. Shoot

    Hero, detail and lifestyle coverage in one efficient schedule.

  4. Roll-out

    Edits sized and paced for every placement.

Typical deliverables

  • Launch hero film
  • Teasers + countdown clips
  • Product-page loops
  • Paid social ad set
  • Vertical reels per product or look

Questions

Can you shoot multiple products in one day?

Yes. We build the schedule around your product list so you get the most usable assets per shoot day.

Do you provide models or talent?

We can cast talent, or feature your community. NuWav's launch starred local creatives instead of models, and it fit the brand perfectly.

Can you make ads in multiple versions for testing?

Yes. We plan alternate hooks, lengths and calls-to-action so your team can A/B test.
