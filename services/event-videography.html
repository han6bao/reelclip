Event videography in Seattle

Corporate events, brand activations, concerts and nightlife, captured as they happen and cut into recaps people actually finish watching.

START A PROJECT →SEE EXAMPLES

Live events don't give second takes. We show up with a shot list, a plan for audio and enough crew to be in the right place when the moment happens. Then we turn it around fast, because recaps matter most while the event is still being talked about.

We've covered a three-event TurboTax campaign (delivered in one week), a multi-day Chilean Salmon culinary program at the University of Washington, Mariah The Scientist at Sarajevo and a full Laid Back Allure weekend.

What's included

  • Event recap films
  • On-site interviews + testimonials
  • Mic setup + live audio capture
  • Multi-camera crews for larger events
  • Event photography
  • Same-week and rush delivery
  • Brand activation + sponsor coverage

How it works

  1. Run of show

    We review your schedule, sponsors and must-have moments.

  2. Crew plan

  4. Fast turnaround

Questions

How fast can we get our event recap?

Turnaround depends on scope, but we regularly deliver within a week. For TurboTax, all three event recaps were delivered inside one week.

Can you cover multiple events or locations?

Yes. We hire and direct additional videographers when events overlap or run across several venues.

Do you record interviews and testimonials?

Yes. We bring microphones and set up clean audio for interviews, presenters and live demos.

Do you also take photos?

Yes. Event photography can be added to any video package.
