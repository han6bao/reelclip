Music videos

Music video production in Seattle + Tacoma

Concept, direction, cinematography, editing and color for artists who want their video to look as good as the record sounds.

START A PROJECT →SEE EXAMPLES

Music videos are where Reelclip's style was built. We've shot at the Washington State Fair, in Seattle lobbies and on escalators, and on late-night streets in Tacoma and Spanaway.

We collaborate on the concept or bring yours to life, then handle the shoot, edit and color. That range is why brands hire us too: it shows we can make something with personality.

What's included

  • Concept development + treatment
  • Location scouting
  • Direction + cinematography
  • Lighting + practical effects
  • Editing + color grading
  • Release cutdowns for social

How it works

  1. Treatment

    The idea, the look and the locations.

  2. Prep

    Schedule, crew, wardrobe and gear.

  3. Shoot

    Performance and story coverage.

  4. Release

    Final video plus teasers for rollout.

Typical deliverables

  • Official music video
  • Vertical teasers + snippets
  • Thumbnail stills
  • Behind-the-scenes content

Questions

How much is a music video?

Budgets vary with locations, crew and shoot days. Share your budget and we'll recommend the strongest concept for it.

Do you shoot outside Seattle?

Yes. We've shot in Tacoma, Spanaway and at the Washington State Fair in Puyallup, and travel across Washington.

Can you help with the concept?

Yes. Some artists bring a full vision; others want us to develop the treatment with them.
