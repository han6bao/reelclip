Tha Baby – Street Runner

ClientTha Baby
ServiceOfficial music video
DeliverablesOfficial music video (2:25)
Year2022

The brief

A colorful, high-energy video for "Street Runner" shot at the Washington State Fair, using the rides and neon as the set.

The result

A familiar public place turned into an energetic, neon-lit music video. Released in May 2022 on Tha Baby's YouTube channel, where it has passed 16,000 views.

“Color, movement, and energy after dark.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

A vibrant music video that captures the track's energy through colorful cinematography and an immersive fairground setting.

The direction

Build on the artist's location concept and planned opening, using the fair's lights, attractions and movement.

The execution

Reelclip and Miguel Manzano shot across the fairgrounds with a hyperlapse intro and performance coverage. Miguel edited; Reelclip graded.

The deliverable

One official music video for "Street Runner".

How we shot it

The artist's fair

Tha Baby brought the location and a planned opening. We built the camera work and the look on top, using the fair itself as the set.

Hyperlapse, then run and gun

A hyperlapse-style intro sets the motion, then we moved through the fairgrounds lit only by the rides and neon.

Glow in the grade

Miguel Manzano cut it to the track's pace, and we graded it with glow, saturation and light treatments to push the neon.
