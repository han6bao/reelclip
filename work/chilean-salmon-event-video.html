Chilean Salmon – Culinary Event Film

ClientChilean Salmon
ServiceEvent recap
Deliverables1:42 promotional film
Year2026

The brief

Turn several days of Chilean salmon cooking demos, talks and interviews into one short film that teaches as much as it promotes.

The result

A 1:42 film that ties several days and venues into one story about Chilean salmon, built to inform and engage.

“From culinary demonstration to conversation, bringing the story of Chilean salmon to the screen.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

An educational promo for Chilean salmon through live cooking demos, presentations, interviews and event footage.

The direction

Follow the client's shot list and educational priorities, and make them visually engaging.

The execution

Multi-day, multi-location coverage with mic'd interviews and audio for the live chef demo.

The deliverable

One 1:42 promotional film with text animations and motion graphics.

How we shot it

The chef demo

Live cooking at the center: the prep, the finished plates and the ingredients up close.

Clean audio first

We brought the mics for interviews and the chef's presentation, since the edit leans on what speakers say.

One story, many rooms

Footage from the University of Washington and a hotel venue cut into one film, with text animations for the key facts.

Scope of work

  • Multi-day, multi-location coverage
  • Client shot list execution
  • Culinary demo cinematography
  • Food + product B-roll
  • Interviews + seminar coverage
  • Mic setup + live audio support
  • Editing + sequencing
  • Text animation + motion graphics
  • Color grading
