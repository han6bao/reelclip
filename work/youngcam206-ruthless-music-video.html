The brief

A performance-driven video for YoungCam206's "Ruthless", built around a vintage car, a freeway overpass, smoke and dramatic light instead of a complicated storyline.

The result

A cinematic music video built from a straightforward, dependable concept, leaving more time for lighting and experimentation. Released in July 2024 on the Reel Clip YouTube channel.

“Raw energy, vintage machinery, and the city after dark.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

A visually distinctive video that complements the track without relying on an elaborate storyline.

The direction

Performance around a vintage car under a freeway overpass, with dramatic lighting and smoke, built on resources we could confirm in advance.

The execution

We developed the concept, scouted the location, built the shot list, ran logistics and directed the shoot, with Roscoe assisting and Ghxstboi as gaffer. We cut it; Gabe graded it.

The deliverable

One official music video for "Ruthless".

How we shot it

Planned around what we could lock in

Instead of a story that depended on things outside our control, we built the concept around a confirmed location, a vintage car, props and lighting.

The car, the overpass, the fog

Wide lenses, a fog machine and multiple lighting setups under the freeway to shape the car's silhouette and fill the frame with atmosphere.

Fast and slow

Fast-motion runs against slower cinematic moments, plus black-and-white and drone footage, all tied together in the grade by Gabe.
