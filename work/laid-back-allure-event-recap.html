Laid Back Allure – Weekend Recap

ClientLaid Back Allure
ServiceEvent recap
DeliverablesVertical social content + 1:00 weekend recap
Year2024

The brief

Document a three-day Laid Back Allure weekend, a brunch, an all-white party and an estate gathering, in clean, real footage that can keep promoting future events.

The result

Vertical social assets plus a 1:00 horizontal film that ties the whole weekend together, filmed in September 2024.

“Three days. Different experiences. One weekend worth remembering.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Promotional coverage of each gathering and of the weekend as a whole.

The direction

Capture the people, the atmosphere and the activities with clean, authentic cinematography.

The execution

Filmed solo across all three events, then edited and color graded in-house.

The deliverable

Vertical social assets and one 1:00 horizontal weekend recap.

How we shot it

Three rooms, three moods

A relaxed brunch, an elevated all-white party and a dance-floor estate night, each shot to fit its own setting.

As it happened

No staged scenes. Guests, style, music and dancing, caught as the weekend unfolded.

One weekend, one film

Short vertical cuts for social, plus a longer recap with one consistent grade across every location.

About Laid Back Allure

Laid Back Allure throws R&B parties around Seattle, best known for its Late Nights R&B series. The nights celebrate the dance floor and karaoke classics that R&B lovers hold dear, and have taken over rooms like Neumos on Capitol Hill and Good Bar in Pioneer Square.
