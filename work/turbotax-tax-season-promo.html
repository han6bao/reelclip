Year2026

The brief

Capture the energy of a TurboTax tax-season street team as it hits the city, with the branded gear, the promo vehicle and the people it meets.

The result

A roughly 60-second promo that documents the campaign from flyers and storefront visits to street conversations, made by a two-person crew in an unpredictable, live setting.

“Capturing a campaign in motion, from the people behind it to the communities it reaches.”Creative statement

How we shot it

No storyboard, just priorities

Minimal preproduction and no formal storyboard. We worked from the client's shot list and reacted to what happened in real time.

Two cameras, one plan

The lead videographer directed on site and tracked every requested shot, while a second camera grabbed supporting B-roll.

Cut fast, graded clean

Edited and color graded into a short, punchy promo that keeps the pace of the day.

Scope of work

  • On-site production direction
  • Primary + secondary cinematography
  • Live shot coordination
  • Branded merch + vehicle coverage
  • Community engagement footage
  • Supporting B-roll
  • Editing + color grading
  • Short-form promo delivery

About TurboTax

TurboTax is Intuit's tax software, used by millions of people to file every year, with in-person stores and local tax experts around the Seattle area.

For tax season, a TurboTax street marketing team hit the city in branded apparel and a promo vehicle, handing out flyers, stopping into local businesses and talking with people along the way.
