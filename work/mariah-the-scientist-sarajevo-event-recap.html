The brief

Recap Mariah The Scientist's night at Sarajevo, and work a luxury rental car feature into the same film without it feeling like an ad.

The result

One fast, music-driven recap that serves the event and the rental partner at once, filmed and finished by a solo Reelclip shooter.

“The artist. The atmosphere. The moments that made the night.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

An engaging recap of Mariah The Scientist at Sarajevo, with the night's energy and a luxury vehicle feature.

The direction

Fast-paced and music-driven, mixing artist coverage, candid moments, live audio and cinematic nightlife footage.

The execution

Filmed solo, following the organizers' schedule and shot list, then edited, sound designed and graded in-house.

The deliverable

One roughly 60-second recap for digital marketing and event promotion.

How we shot it

Two brands, one film

It opens on the rental partner's yellow Lamborghini Urus in the alley, then hands off to the night itself.

Solo and on the move

One shooter, no second takes. The organizers handled the artist so we could be in the right spot as moments happened.

Raw audio, quick cuts

Live sound, short voice clips and sound effects drive the edit, with the grade holding it together under club lighting.

Scope of work

  • Solo event videography
  • Live artist + nightlife coverage
  • Shot priority coordination
  • Luxury vehicle promo footage
  • Multi-brand integration
  • Music selection
  • Editing + sequencing
  • Sound effects + live audio
  • Color grading

About Mariah The Scientist

Mariah The Scientist is an R&B singer-songwriter from Atlanta. Her 2025 album Hearts Sold Separately debuted at No. 1 on Billboard's Top R&B Albums chart, and its single "Burning Blue" became her biggest hit yet, reaching the Top 30 of the Hot 100 and going 2x Platinum.

Sarajevo Restaurant & Lounge serves Balkan, Greek and Turkish small plates by day, with a patio looking out over Puget Sound. At night it turns into one of downtown's late-night spots, with DJs most nights of the week.
