Peristera – First Collection Launch

ClientPeristera
ServiceLaunch campaign + reels
Deliverables1 campaign film + 5 promo reels
Year—

The brief

Launch Peristera's first-ever collection with stylized campaign content that matches the brand's vision and builds excitement for the drop.

The result

Six launch-ready videos, one campaign film and five reels, that turned Peristera's references into content for its debut collection.

“The first drop. A new identity in motion.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Introduce Peristera's first apparel collection with content made to support the launch.

The direction

Follow the brand's own references, music and concept, with an expressive, fashion-first look.

The execution

Film, light and edit the whole campaign solo, with careful product coverage and stylized post.

The deliverables

One main campaign film and five reels: six assets for the launch and ads.

How we shot it

The brand's vision

Peristera brought the references, music and style. Our job was turning that concept into shots.

Lit for the apparel

Multiple lighting setups and intentional framing, so every print and fabric reads on camera.

Edit-heavy by design

Dynamic pacing, creative transitions and layered effects pull the six videos together.

Scope of work

  • Campaign cinematography
  • Lighting setup + execution
  • Client-directed visual production
  • Apparel + product coverage
  • Stylized video editing
  • Creative transitions + visual effects
  • Short-form ad content
  • Final video delivery

About Peristera

Peristera is a new apparel brand that came into its first collection with a clear creative vision: big back graphics led by its dove and NOT MONEY print, plus a puff-print graffiti logo.

The debut collection covers T-shirts, sweaters, hoodies and crewnecks, in colorways from cobalt and black to charcoal, brown and cream.
