Rell Moore – Chrome Hearts

ClientRell Moore
ServiceOfficial music video
DeliverablesOfficial music video (2:20)
Year2023

The brief

A black-and-white video for Rell Moore's "Chrome Hearts" that lets Seattle's architecture, textures and contrast carry the performance.

The result

A distinctive urban video made without an elaborate narrative or big production setup. Released in April 2023 on the Reel Clip YouTube channel.

“The city as a backdrop. Contrast as the language.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

A black-and-white music video that captures an urban aesthetic through performance, city architecture and experimental effects.

The direction

Follow the artist's concept while finding Seattle locations with strong architecture, contrasting textures and a raw city feel.

The execution

We filmed across Seattle in lobbies, on escalators, at construction sites and graffiti walls, shooting flexibly and composing in real time, then coordinated with Mr. Coy on the edit and graphics.

The deliverable

One official music video for "Chrome Hearts".

How we shot it

The artist's concept

Rell Moore came in with a black-and-white, city-inspired concept and a preferred location. Our job was turning that into the finished video.

Run and gun through Seattle

No storyboard or set shot list: we found escalators, corridors, construction sites and graffiti walls as we went, composing in real time.

A graphic finish

Mr. Coy edited the video and added the graphic effects, with Reelclip guiding the look to match the artist's vision.

About Rell Moore

"Chrome Hearts" is named after the luxury jewelry and fashion house that has become a staple in hip-hop style. Rell Moore set the concept and a starting location, and the video was shot across Seattle in 2023, premiering on the Reel Clip YouTube channel that April.
