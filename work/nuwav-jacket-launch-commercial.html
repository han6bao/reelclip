NuWav – Jacket Launch Commercial

ClientNuWav
ServiceJacket launch commercial
Deliverables45-second commercial for web, Instagram + ads
Year2024

The brief

Launch NuWav's newest jacket by putting it on Seattle's creatives, not models, and tying the brand to the city's music and youth culture.

The result

A 45-second commercial that turned a jacket launch into a snapshot of Seattle's creative culture, running on NuWav's website, Instagram and digital ads.

“Made for the ones creating their own wave.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Build buzz for NuWav's jacket with a commercial that stands out across its digital platforms.

The direction

A fashion campaign built around Seattle's creative youth and recognizable local talent.

The execution

We developed the concept, scouted talent and locations, directed the shoot, and cut it with stylized editing, color and VFX.

The deliverable

One 45-second commercial for the website, Instagram, social and digital ads.

How we shot it

Creatives, not models

We scouted musicians, content creators and rising talent from Seattle's creative scene and built each scene around them.

Each in their element

In the studio, at their streaming setups and out on the street, lit and framed differently for each person, with no fashion posing.

Edited to stand out

Expressive pacing, color and VFX tie every creative and location into one film made for Instagram.

  • Location scouting

About NuWav

NuWav makes streetwear and apparel. For its latest jacket, the brand wanted more than a product ad: a film that placed the jacket inside Seattle's creative community.

The campaign features local creatives Kid Steez, Xavier Weeks and D. Hayes, each filmed in their own world, from recording in the studio to streaming setups and outdoor performances.
