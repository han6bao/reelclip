Zadart – Exotic Car Campaign

ClientZadart
ServiceSocial ad campaign
DeliverablesSocial-first promo for Instagram + paid ads
Year2026

The brief

Make two new Lamborghinis in Zadart's rental collection stop the scroll, with a high-energy, music-video-style promo built for Instagram.

The result

A social-first automotive promo, filmed in March 2026, that puts Zadart's Lamborghinis on Seattle's streets and closes on the Zadart Exotic Car Club & Rentals logo.

“Luxury in motion. Built to turn heads.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Promote two Lamborghinis from Zadart's rental collection with social-first ad content.

The direction

A fast, music-video-inspired campaign about the cars' design, movement and presence around Seattle.

The execution

We built the concept, picked the music, scouted locations, shot it, and cut and graded it, with aerial and FPV drone by Cris City.

The deliverable

A promo video for Instagram and digital advertising.

How we shot it

Creative freedom

Zadart asked for something that stands out on social. We set the concept, the music, the locations and how the cars would be shown.

Rolling, aerial + FPV

Rolling car-to-car footage, dynamic angles and drone work, including FPV passes that track the cars up close at speed.

Cut to the beat

Rhythmic transitions and stylized effects synced to the track, paced to grab attention in the first few seconds.

Scope of work

  • Creative direction + concept development
  • Visual planning + location scouting
  • Music selection + visual pacing
  • Automotive cinematography
  • Rolling vehicle coverage
  • Aerial + FPV drone cinematography
  • Production coordination
  • Editing + sequencing
  • Stylized transitions + effects
  • Color grading
  • Social media ad content

About Zadart

Zadart started with one leased BMW, when founder Jonny Netz was 17 and still in high school on the Eastside. It grew into a Seattle-area fleet of exotic and luxury cars, built mostly on word of mouth and five-star reviews.

Today it runs as Zadart Exotic Car Club & Rentals, handling rentals, delivery and detailing, so the car shows up ready for the occasion.
