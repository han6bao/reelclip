Music video direction + production

TBG Kam Bino – My Life

ClientTBG Kam Bino
ServiceOfficial music video
DeliverablesOfficial music video (1:35)
Year2022

The brief

Turn the darker themes of "My Life" into a cinematic, story-driven video of the artist's world, and keep that vision intact when the shoot day got cut short.

The result

A polished, performance-centered film despite a shortened shoot. Released in March 2022 on the Reel Clip YouTube channel, it's one of the channel's most-watched videos, with 17,000+ views.

“An exploration of life through shadows, movement, and the city.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

A cinematic music video that translates the song's darker themes into a visual story of the artist's experiences and identity.

The direction

An atmospheric, story-driven concept built on urban locations, dramatic lighting, performance and a desaturated palette with cool blue undertones.

The execution

We planned the treatment, mood boards and locations, then adapted on the day, shooting, lighting, cutting and grading with Miguel Manzano. Aerials by @luhdarb.

The deliverable

One official music video for "My Life".

How we shot it

Planned to the frame

We built a mood board, vision board and narrative treatment, and scouted urban locations to match the song's darker tone.

Under the overpass

When scheduling cut the shoot short, we prioritized the key performance scenes, and the overpass became the video's defining location.

Shadows and blue

Edited and graded with Miguel Manzano: deep shadows, high contrast, muted color and cool blue undertones.
