Gerard Cycles – Brand Film

ClientGerard Cycles
ServiceBrand film + website hero
Deliverables60-second website hero film
Year2026

The brief

A cinematic hero film for the website that shows how the bikes are built and how they feel to ride, not a corporate commercial.

The result

A 60-second hero film that opens the Gerard Cycles website, filmed in December 2025 and launched in April 2026.

“Built by hand. Dialed with intention. Ridden the way it's meant to be.”Creative statement

How we shot it

Built from the ground up

With no brand style guide to follow, we created the look from scratch: mood board, storyboard, shot list, pacing and color.

Two days, ground and air

A two-day shoot with pro cinema cameras and drone, from the hands building each frame to the bikes at speed on the road.

Cut for the homepage

Edited, paced and color graded into a 60-second film made to play at the top of the website.

Scope of work

  • Creative direction + visual concept
  • Mood board + storyboard
  • Shot list + production planning
  • Two-day cinematography
  • Drone footage
  • Editing, pacing + color grading
  • Final 60-second website hero film

About Gerard Cycles

Gerard Cycles builds its own road and gravel bikes, including the carbon Force 100 and the Trail Force RX, each handcrafted in Kirkland. Founder John Gerard Sheehan oversees every fit himself.

The shop also does custom builds, service and upgrades, and keeps the local riding community going with workshops, group rides, race support and youth programs for riders ages 9 to 17.
