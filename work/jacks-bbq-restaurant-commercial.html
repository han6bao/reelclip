About Jack's BBQ

Jack's BBQ is a Texas-style barbecue restaurant in Seattle, founded by Jack Timmons, a Dallas native and Texas A&M graduate who trained at the university's barbecue camp before opening his first location. The original roadhouse in SoDo, on Airport Way South, is known for oak-smoked brisket cooked low and slow in steel offset smokers over Texas post oak and Washington applewood.

The menu covers the Texas classics: brisket, spare ribs and smoked sausage on the Texas Trinity plate, plus breakfast tacos, Texas chili, corn pudding and pecan pie. Jack's has grown to several locations around the Puget Sound, including South Lake Union and Redmond, and works with local philanthropic partners throughout the year.
