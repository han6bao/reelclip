Lula Coffee Co. – Brand Film

ClientLula Coffee Co.
ServiceBrand film + social ad
Deliverables30-second promotional film for social
Year2024

The brief

Show that Lula is more than a place to grab a drink: a pink, thoughtfully designed space to gather, stay a while and take photos.

The result

A 30-second film filmed in August 2024 for social media and brand marketing, introducing Lula as a place worth visiting, not just a coffee stop.

“More than coffee. A space worth experiencing.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Build awareness of Lula Coffee with a social ad people actually stop and watch.

The direction

Lean into the pink aesthetic Lula already has, the inviting interior and the signature drinks.

The execution

A clean, atmospheric 30-second film that feels like walking in the door.

The deliverable

One promotional film for social media and brand marketing.

How we shot it

Working within the brand

Lula already had a clear look, so we scouted the space and built a mood board and shot list around it.

Slow, clean frames

Intentional framing and slower pacing, no flashy transitions, so the colors and architecture do the talking.

Drinks as the accent

A few close-up product shots, like the pink pour and the caramel drizzle, to round out the space.

Scope of work

  • Creative direction
  • Location scouting + visual assessment
  • Mood board + shot list
  • Interior + product cinematography
  • Brand-aligned visual storytelling
  • Editing + color grading
  • 30-second promotional film for social

About Lula Coffee Co.

Coffee industry veteran Taylor Platt opened Lula Coffee Co. in West Seattle in 2020, with a cheerful cafe and a drive-thru, and it thrived through the pandemic. A second cafe followed in SoDo in 2023, on Airport Way South by the Old Rainier Brewery's giant R sign.

The SoDo shop is all hot-pink counters and walls, puffy clouds on the ceiling and a plush blue couch, made to feel bright on gray Seattle days. The menu covers coffee classics, teas, blended drinks and smoothies, with collagen- and vegetable-powder boosted lattes and playful drinks like Wake & Bake and Don't Huck With Me.
