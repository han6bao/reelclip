TurboTax – Three-Event Campaign

ClientTurboTax
ServiceEvent campaign
Deliverables3 event recaps + photography, delivered in one week
Year2026

The brief

Cover three TurboTax activations around Seattle and Bellevue during tax season, Sneaker Con, a community pickleball day and a new location's grand opening, and deliver all three recaps within one week.

The result

Three event recaps plus photography, all delivered inside the one-week turnaround, with Reelclip directing a crew of three videographers and an editor.

“Three activations. One production team. Delivered in a week.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Promotional coverage for three TurboTax activations, supporting the brand's Bellevue-area rollout during tax season.

The direction

Client-led. TurboTax set the branding, shot lists and campaign direction for every event.

The execution

Reelclip ran it as Production Director: hired the videographers and editor, assigned each event, filmed two of them and managed the handoff to post.

The deliverables

Three promotional recaps and event photography, all delivered within one week.

About TurboTax

TurboTax is Intuit's tax software, used by millions of people to file every year, with in-person stores and local tax experts around the Seattle area.

For tax season, TurboTax showed up in person: a booth at Sneaker Con, the city's one-day sneaker convention, a community pickleball activation in Bellevue, and the grand opening of a new Bellevue location with florals, live violin and a photo station.
