Saint Meadow – Can't Complain

ClientSaint Meadow
ServiceOfficial music video
DeliverablesOfficial music video
Year2024

The brief

When the planned house party fell through on shoot day, turn "Can't Complain" into a darker, atmospheric performance video on the spot.

The result

A dark, cinematic performance video that held together through a last-minute change of plans, with no drop in quality or visual consistency.

“When the plan changed, the vision evolved.”Creative statement

Approach

The objective

Translate Saint Meadow's identity and the song's energy into a visually engaging music video.

The direction

Planned as a house party, the concept shifted to a darker, more atmospheric performance video when the day required it.

The execution

We adapted on location with multiple lighting setups, gimbal moves, car-mounted shots and expressive camera work, then cut it with VFX. Gabe handled color.

The deliverable

One official music video for "Can't Complain".

How we shot it

The house

The artist's own house became the main set, lit for a darker, moodier feel than the original house-party plan.

Under the spotlight

Outdoor scenes under a spotlight setup, plus vehicle-mounted shots, added contrast and depth.

Built in the edit

With the original storyline gone, the edit leans on rhythm, performance and VFX, with color by Gabe.
